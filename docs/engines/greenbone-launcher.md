# Greenbone launcher

The Go entrypoint of the [Greenbone](greenbone.md) image. It validates the scope, resolves the VT selection from the embedded feed, runs a private openvasd, relays the scanner's connections to the exact target through the managed SOCKS gateway, validates every result, and writes the only evidence file, `/output/greenbone.xml`.

## How it is wired

- **Contract.** Arguments must be exactly `--engine greenbone --scope /run/ai-security-scanner/scope.json --output /output`. `AI_SECURITY_SCANNER_PROXY` must be `socks5h://<gateway>:1080` with an IPv4 literal and no user, path, query or fragment (`managedProxy`). Any error prints `Greenbone engine launcher: <reason>` and exits 126; cancellation exits 130.
- **Scope** (`loadScope`, `validateAndPlan`, `validateGrant`). At most 4 MiB; unknown and trailing JSON is rejected.
  - `generated_at` set and at most 5 minutes ahead. Assets of kind `domain`, `host`, `ip_address` or `web_service`, without provider or region, with 1 to 128 identifiers that all equal the canonical target and 1 to 16 grants. All grants must belong to one case.
  - Each grant: permission `active_external_testing`, an `authorization_reference`, an external policy that matches the grant (id, asset, approver, timestamps), expiry in the future and at most 30 days after approval, activity `active_external`, protocol `tcp`, `tls`, `http` or `https`, a canonical lowercase hostname or canonical IP (no wildcard, no network range), 1 to 1024 sorted unique ports, 1 to 10 requests per second, concurrency 1 to 5, timeout 1 to 3600 s.
  - Template policy (`validateTemplatePolicy`): headless, out-of-band, fuzzing, file upload, denial of service and credential attacks all false; `revision` equal to `greenbone-community-feed@<feedRevision>`; exactly one of `profile_id` (only `greenbone_remote_safe_v1`) or `allowed_template_ids` (valid unique OIDs, at most 128 per grant and 512 per run).
- **Selection** (`loadFeedIndex`, `resolveTemplateSelection`). Reads `/opt/greenbone/feed/vt-metadata.json` (at most 256 MiB and 200,000 VTs, unique OIDs and filenames).
  - `greenbone_remote_safe_v1` (`deriveRemoteSafeSelection`): every VT of category `gather_info` that is not `tag.deprecated` and whose family is not excluded by `excludedRemoteSafeFamily` (suffix ` Local Security Checks`, prefix `Nmap NSE`, and Brute force attacks, Default Accounts, Credentials, Policy, Compliance, the three IT-Grundschutz families, Port scanners). An empty selection is an error. It is computed once per run.
  - `validateSafeSelectionBounded`: directly selected VTs must be `gather_info`; the dependency closure may contain only `gather_info`, `settings`, `init`, `scanner` and `end`; a cycle or unresolved dependency filename fails. The connect-based TCP scanner (`1.3.6.1.4.1.25623.1.0.10335`, `2011/openvas_tcp_scanner.nasl`, category `scanner`) is always added.
- **openvasd** (`startOpenvasd`, `waitReady`, `stopProcessGroup`). Private 0700 temp directory, random 32-byte API key, own process group, fixed environment. Flags: `--scanner-type openvasd --storage-type inmemory --feed-path /opt/greenbone/feed`, Notus `--advisories` and `--products`, `--listening 127.0.0.1:3000 --feed-check-interval 86400 --max-running-scans 1 --max-queued-scans 1 --check_interval 1 --log-level WARN --feed-signature-check`. Ready when `GET /vts` returns 200 (503 until both signed feeds load), within 5 minutes. Shutdown is SIGTERM to the group, then SIGKILL after 5 s.
- **Relays** (`startUnitRelays`, `serve`, `dialManagedSOCKS`). Per grant, one `tcp4` listener on `127.0.0.2:<approved port>`, so NASL keeps the real port number while staying off the API address. Only loopback clients are accepted; connections are limited to the grant's concurrency and spaced by 1 s divided by its rate. Each becomes a SOCKS5 no-auth CONNECT to the exact target and port (a hostname is sent as a domain name), with the grant timeout capped at 60 s for the handshake.
- **Scan** (`buildScanRequest`, `runUnit`). One scan per grant, sorted by asset id then grant id. Host `127.0.0.2`, one TCP range per relay port, VTs = selection plus the TCP scanner, no credentials, alive test `consider_alive`. Preferences include `safe_checks yes`, `auto_enable_dependencies yes`, `max_hosts 1`, `max_checks` = concurrency, `checks_read_timeout` = grant timeout, `time_between_request` = ceil(1000 / rate) ms, `open_sock_max_attempts 1`, `timeout_retry 0`, `expand_vhosts no`, `table_driven_lsc no`, `max_mem_kb 128`; the grant timeout is kept out of `plugins_timeout`. `POST /scans` (201) and start (204), then `/status` every 250 ms: `succeeded` reads `/results`; `failed`, `stopped` or an unknown state fails. Deadline: the earlier of grant expiry and 2 hours. The scan is stopped if needed and deleted on exit. The container timeout (catalog 7200 s) bounds the whole run.
- **Results** (`results`, `resultCarriesAdapterEvidence`, `validateResult`). The body must be an array or an object with a non-null `items` field, at most 100,000 results. OID-less `log`, `host_start`, `host_end`, `host_stop` and `host_detail` records are dropped. Every other result needs a known type, an IP that is empty or the relay address, and, unless it is an OID-less `error` or `dead_host`, an OID present in the feed with a safe category. An `alarm` must be in the validated closure, on an approved relay port, over TCP; a host-level (port 0) alarm therefore fails the run. One invalid result fails the whole run.
- **Output** (`writeXMLResult`). `/output/greenbone.xml`, created with `O_EXCL` and mode 0600 behind a 512 MiB bounded writer, and removed unless every grant completes. Per result: `name`, `host` (the original target), `port` (`<approved>/tcp`, or `0/tcp` for host-level), `result_type`, `severity`, `threat`, `asset_id`, `summary` and `solution` from the feed, `description` (the result message), `raw_host`, `raw_port`, `relay_mapping`, `scope_grant_id`, then `<qod><value>` when known and `<nvt oid>` with name, family and up to 32 CVE refs.

## Downstream changes

Only openvasd and the feed ship, not the full Greenbone stack ([Greenbone components](../engine-catalog.md#greenbone-components)), so the launcher does the translation a manager would otherwise do:

- **Severity.** The openvasd result the launcher decodes (`scanResult`) has no severity. For an `alarm`, `writeXMLResult` computes the CVSS 3.x base score from `tag.severity_vector` (`cvssBaseScore`), else the CVSS 2 score from `tag.cvss_base_vector` (`cvss2BaseScore`), and labels it with `threatForScore`. A CVSS 4.0 or unparsable vector leaves the alarm unrated (`0.0`, threat `Unknown`); it is never turned into `Log`. Other types are written as `0.0` and `Log`.
- **QoD.** `qodForType` maps six `tag.qod_type` values: exploit 100, remote_vul 99, package 97, remote_active 95, remote_banner 80, remote_banner_unreliable 30. Any other value omits `<qod>` and prints a stderr warning (4ec35b0).
- **Profile policy.** The remote-safe category rule and the English family-name exclusions are product scan policy. Changing them changes coverage; the open owner decisions are listed in [Product decisions intentionally not implemented](../greenbone-refresh-analysis.md#product-decisions-intentionally-not-implemented).
- **Address hiding.** The scanner sees only `127.0.0.2`; `relay_mapping` records `managed-socks5:127.0.0.2:<port> -> <target>:<port>`.

## Lessons from real runs

- 2026-10-01: with one CPU, openvasd starved its status API and the launcher's 30 s request timeout ended the scan with exit 126 -> the reviewed CPU allowance is now applied (2ddaec2). See [Greenbone](greenbone.md#lessons-from-real-runs) for the image-level lessons.
- Hardening that came from review rather than a failed run: unrated alarms stay alarms and OID-less `error` and `dead_host` results are kept (369a6b9, 2026-09-09); an unknown OID-less result type and a missing or null `items` field now fail instead of reading as a clean scan (c668b6d, 2026-09-17); unrecognized QoD types are omitted instead of written as 50 (4ec35b0, 2026-09-17).

## Product execution isolation

The launcher publishes one atomic XML file and removes it on any failed grant. The product therefore plans **one Greenbone task per exact grant**, each with its own engine-run ID, evidence directory and cleanup identity. `execution_scope_grant_ids` freezes the task's grant membership; resume selects only those grants and revalidates the original scope hash. A failed task cannot delete a completed sibling's output, including siblings on the same asset.

Historical multi-grant executions may reopen their saved reports and finish adapting already captured evidence. They cannot replay the old atomic batch; Start a new scan creates independent tasks. Do not restore multi-grant batching or describe the launcher's raw batch mode as partial-result safe. This isolation changes host orchestration, not OpenVAS detection or the published image.

## Updating this launcher

- Any change to `main.go` needs a new `wrapper.launcher_sha256` in the plan and therefore a new image tag; follow [Greenbone](greenbone.md#updating-this-engine).
- `validateGreenboneBuildClosure` matches `feedRevision             = "<FEED_COMMIT>"` and `managedGatewayPort       = "1080"` byte for byte, including gofmt's column alignment, plus the `managedProxy(os.Getenv("AI_SECURITY_SCANNER_PROXY"))` call, `byRelayPort` and `byOriginalPort`. A longer constant name added to that block realigns the lines and fails validation.
- **openvasd API.** On a scanner update, re-check `POST /scans` (201 with a UUID), `POST /scans/<id>` with `{"action":"start"}` (204) and `stop`, `/status` and its states, `/results` and its envelope, `DELETE`, `GET /vts` readiness, the result fields `id`, `type`, `ip_address`, `oid`, `port`, `protocol` and `message`, and the command-line flags above. Unknown states and result types fail closed by design.
- **Feed metadata.** Re-check the `vt-metadata.json` fields in `vtMetadata` and `metadataTag`: `oid`, `name`, `filename`, `category`, `family`, `dependencies`, `references`, `tag.cvss_base_vector`, `tag.severity_vector`, `tag.qod_type`, `tag.summary`, `tag.solution`, `tag.deprecated`. Go ignores unknown fields, so a rename is silent: it can empty remediation, re-include deprecated tests, or bypass a family exclusion.
- **XML contract.** The field names are bound on the adapter side by `greenbone_xml_contract_binds_launcher_field_names` in `src-tauri/src/adapters/mod.rs`; change both sides together.
- **Tests.** 24 Go tests in `main_test.go` run in the image build, for example `TestRemoteSafeProfileDerivesSortedGatherInfoSelection`, `TestFeedSelectionClosesOnlySafeDependencies`, `TestUnitRelayUsesExactSOCKSConnect`, `TestBuildScanRequestKeepsGrantTimeoutOutOfVTLifetimePreferences`, `TestValidateResultSeparatesAlarmClosureFromSafeSystemLogs`, `TestWriteXMLResultKeepsUnratedAlarmAuthoritative` and `TestWriteXMLResultOmitsUnknownQODAndWarnsOnce`. The workflow smoke (`managed-socks-smoke.sh`) is the only end-to-end check of relays and SOCKS.
- **Compare with raw output.** The `relay_mapping` and `raw_port` fields in `greenbone.xml` show which relay port each result came from; `port` must equal the approved port it maps to. The launcher's warnings for unrecognized QoD types are in the run's captured stderr.
